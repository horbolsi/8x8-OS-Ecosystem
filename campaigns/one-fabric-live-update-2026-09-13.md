8x8 is converging its historical interfaces, dashboards, Studio systems, agents, memory, device fabric, World/360 surfaces, Vault concepts, research tools and command infrastructure into one governed product under `fabric://8x8/core`.

## One interface, three gates

- **Gate 1 — Public World:** public discovery, demos, Studio showcases, World/360, agents, research, content, marketplace discovery and public proof surfaces.
- **Gate 2 — Identity & Connection:** 8x8 ID, account, recovery, Vault, connectors, models, social/mail, devices, subscriptions and tenant permissions.
- **Gate 3 — Sovereign Workspace:** Command Center, Studio/OpenCut, Agent Council, Jarvis/voice, Memory/Knowledge Graph, Art Board 360, Hands/device fabric, research, content operations, observability and bounded owner tools.

## What we are doing now

We are recovering useful capabilities from old Wix, Replit, Vercel, R/V-series, local dashboards, archived code, Studio artifacts and device/runtime systems, then deduplicating and binding them to one capability registry rather than maintaining competing products.

A capability is not marked live merely because a page renders or a process exists. Promotion requires a real backend/tool result, visible output, persistence/recovery and an evidence receipt.

## Truth and safety boundary

Historical visuals and concepts are preserved as design and research provenance, but unsupported revenue, profit, user-count, AGI, token-sale or performance claims are not presented as facts. High-risk financial, signing, mainnet, trading and privileged device effects remain explicitly gated until their required evidence and approvals exist.
