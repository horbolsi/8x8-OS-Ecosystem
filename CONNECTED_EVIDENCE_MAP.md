## Termux, Ubuntu PRoot, and Android internal storage

These environments are described in historical mission reports and continuity documents, but this chat does not currently have a live shell or filesystem connector into them. Their state must be verified through one of the following:

1. a dedicated live connector,
2. a current signed/sanitized audit export,
3. a user-executed evidence bundle,
4. or a reviewed relay path that exposes bounded read-only status.

Historical logs may guide the audit, but they cannot substitute for current service, process, filesystem, repository, secret, and deployment evidence.

## Evidence rule

Connected documents, screenshots, summaries, agent narratives, and previous completion reports are treated as leads. A capability is marked verified only when the current implementation, execution evidence, environment, and limitations agree.
