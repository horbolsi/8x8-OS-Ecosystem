# 8x8 OS Platform Scope

8x8 OS is larger than a single Web3 application or agent demo. It is an evolving human-governed operations fabric spanning public product surfaces, private owner infrastructure, distributed runtimes, communications, intelligence, media, and controlled automation.

## Complete capability domains

1. **Owner governance and authority** — role-based access, approval gates, bounded autonomy, leases, emergency stop, audit receipts, and fail-closed controls.
2. **Agent society and coordination** — Hermes-led orchestration, specialist agents, handoffs, debate, task routing, continuity, and owner escalation.
3. **Memory and knowledge** — durable memories, agent profiles, knowledge graphs, state stores, thread continuity, evidence reconciliation, and safe export mirrors.
4. **Mission and control fabric** — scheduled missions, persistent lanes, heartbeats, health supervision, bounded recovery, hashes, receipts, and progress reporting.
5. **Communications fabric** — Telegram, Discord, relay channels, owner briefings, bot fleets, and external notification surfaces.
6. **Studio and publishing** — text, image, audio, speech, multilingual content, video-oriented workflows, content planning, and publishing coordination.
7. **Trading intelligence and risk** — market monitoring, research, live public feeds, bounded risk policies, strategy analysis, and strictly gated execution.
8. **Blockchain and Web3** — wallets, tokens, NFTs, governance concepts, smart contracts, staking concepts, blockchain intelligence, and multi-chain research.
9. **Developer and repository intelligence** — GitHub discovery, repository maps, safe mirrors, commit intelligence, CI evidence, issue/PR workflows, and deployment artifacts.
10. **Distributed runtime mesh** — Android/Termux, Ubuntu PRoot, phones, Replit, Codespaces, cloud services, containers, and device relay channels.
11. **Security, privacy, and recovery** — secrets isolation, redaction, access control, threat modeling, monitoring, recovery limits, quarantine rules, and forensic audit trails.
12. **Commercial platform layer** — managed deployments, enterprise support, module marketplace concepts, training, consulting, edge-AI packaging, and subscription models.

## Public versus private boundary

The public repositories explain architecture, safe demos, evidence, contribution paths, and verified capabilities. Private repositories and device stores may contain operational state, credentials, agent memories, proprietary logic, trading material, personal data, or incomplete experiments and must never be copied wholesale into public repositories.

## Claim maturity

Every public capability must be labelled as one of:

- **Verified public implementation**
- **Verified private implementation with sanitized evidence**
- **Experimental implementation**
- **Prototype or simulation**
- **Design specification**
- **Planned**

No feature is considered production-ready merely because a UI, route, schema, agent name, document, or mock response exists.
