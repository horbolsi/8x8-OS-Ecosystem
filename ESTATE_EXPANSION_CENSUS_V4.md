## Scale correction

The connected GitHub estate contains 15 repositories and 181 branches in the current universal-context census. With six non-repository authority domains, the first resolver must account for at least 187 authority keys before expanding services, databases, connectors, storage partitions, agents, models, receipts, and generated artifacts.

A repository default branch is not the whole repository. A plan, draft PR, fixture, simulator, historical receipt, or configured connector is not a live capability.

## Canonical product boundary

8x8 converges toward exactly two product lanes:

1. **Private 8x8 Core** — owner authority, local runtime, control fabric, private memory, credentials, services, missions, evidence, recovery, and restricted laboratories.
2. **Public 8x8 User Edition** — sanitized clients, public-safe agents, identity, entitlement, community, educational, marketplace, media, world, and protocol surfaces.

No public client may inherit private owner authority, secret stores, raw memory, unrestricted terminal access, wallets, treasury controls, or production mutation rights.
